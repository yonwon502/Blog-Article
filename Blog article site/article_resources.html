Top 10 Free Resources for Learning English

Published on: May 18, 2026

You don't need to spend a fortune to achieve fluency in English. The internet is packed with high-quality, free resources designed to help learners of all levels. From grammar exercises to listening practice, here are the top 10 free resources you should bookmark today.

1. Duolingo

Duolingo gamifies the language learning experience. It is perfect for beginners and intermediate learners who want to build vocabulary and understand basic sentence structures through quick, bite-sized daily lessons.

2. BBC Learning English

A comprehensive platform offering free audio, video, and text materials. Their resources cover everything from workplace English to vocabulary related to current global news events.

3. Coursera & edX (Free Audits)

While earning a certificate costs money, you can audit hundreds of university-level English courses for free on Coursera and edX. These are excellent for academic and business English.

4. YouTube Channels (e.g., Learn English with Emma)

YouTube is a goldmine for language learners. Channels like "Learn English with Emma" or "EnglishClass101" offer thousands of hours of free video content on grammar, pronunciation, and slang.

5. Project Gutenberg

If you want to improve your reading comprehension, Project Gutenberg offers over 60,000 free eBooks. Reading classic literature in English is a fantastic way to expose yourself to advanced vocabulary and complex sentence structures.

6. TED Talks

TED Talks are brilliant for improving your listening skills and expanding your vocabulary on specific topics. Use the interactive transcripts and subtitles to follow along with the speakers.

7. HelloTalk & Tandem

These language exchange apps connect you with native English speakers who want to learn your native language. It’s a free and practical way to practice conversational English via text or voice notes.

8. Grammarly (Free Version)

While technically a writing assistant, Grammarly's free version is an excellent tool to learn from your mistakes. It highlights your grammatical errors and explains why they are incorrect, helping you improve your writing over time.

9. Voice of America (VOA) Learning English

VOA Learning English provides news stories, podcasts, and videos specifically designed for English learners. The content is spoken at a slightly slower pace, making it perfect for intermediate listeners.

10. Cambridge English Free Activities

Cambridge English offers dozens of free, quick online activities to practice reading, writing, listening, and grammar, categorized by difficulty level.

Conclusion

With these free tools at your disposal, the only thing you need to invest is your time. Choose two or three resources that fit your learning style and incorporate them into your daily routine!
